Background

From quantum physics
to industrial AI.

My background combines a PhD in Physics from the Technical University of Vienna, academic research across Europe, and more than nine years in industrial R&D. I work across machine learning, computer vision, algorithms and software delivery, with a focus on making complex technology useful.

At X-Rite in Regensdorf, I have developed models for automotive paint search and formulation, brought algorithms into cloud and embedded environments, and coordinated software delivery across international teams. I work closely with customers, sales, product managers and engineers to turn requirements into technical priorities.

I combine hands-on implementation with technical guidance and mentoring. My approach is to establish a baseline, understand the tradeoffs, and build evidence through reproducible experiments and evaluation.
